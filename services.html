Our Speciality Treatments

Comprehensive, high-tech dental care tailored for you and your family.

ጥርስ መሙላት (Filling)

Restoration of decayed or damaged teeth using high-quality tooth-colored filling materials.

ጥርስ ማውጣት / መንቀል (Tooth Extraction)

Safe and gentle removal of severely decayed, damaged, or problematic teeth.

ጥርስ ማጽዳት (Cleaning)

Removal of plaque and tartar buildup to protect gums and preserve bright dental hygiene.

ጥርስ መትከል (Implant)

Permanent, natural-looking tooth replacement solutions for missing teeth.

ጥርስ ማስተካከል (Orthodontics)

Straightening misaligned teeth and correcting bite patterns for a balanced smile.

የጥርስ ስር ህክምና (Root Canal Treatment)

Pain-free restoration of damaged or infected inner tooth canals to save natural tooth structure.

ዴንቸር (ወጭ ገቢ ጥርስ) (Denture)

Custom removable replacement teeth designed to restore function and aesthetics.

የህፃናት ጥርስ ህክምና (Pediatric Dentistry)

Specialized, gentle dental care catering specifically to infants, children, and teens.

የተሰበረ መንጋጋ መጠገን (Jaw Fracture Repair)

Surgical and clinical management of jawbone trauma and fractures.

የተወላገዱና የአበቃቀል ችግር ያለባቸውን ጥርሶች በብሬስ ማስተካከል (Orthodontics for Crowding)

Specialized braces treatment to correct overcrowded, twisted, or irregularly erupting teeth.

መጥፎ የአፍ ጠረን ህክምና (Bad Breath Treatment)

Diagnostic and therapeutic solutions to permanently eliminate halitosis and gum odors.

መለስተኛ የአፍ ውስጥ ቀዶ ጥገና (Minor Oral Surgery)

Expert minor surgical procedures inside the oral cavity performed with maximum comfort.
